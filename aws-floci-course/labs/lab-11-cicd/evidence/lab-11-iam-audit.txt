usms-lambda-exec-role
    permissions boundary : NONE
    ACCEPTED (HIGH) trust policy: service principal edgelambda.amazonaws.com with NO Condition (confused deputy)
         reason      : Lambda@Edge (Lab 10): CloudFront replicates and runs the function as edgelambda.amazonaws.com. Which source keys it sends is not verified, and a condition on a key that is never sent would stop every edge function. The lambda.amazonaws.com statement keeps its Lab 09 SourceAccount and SourceArn conditions.
         compensating: The role's only permissions are USMSLambdaBasic (log writes, s3:GetObject); the edge functions get no other permission and USMSDeployBase may pass roles to ecs-tasks only.
